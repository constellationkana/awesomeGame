# awesomeGame
- original idea: 3D Game on Unity, R.E.P.O inspired physics, First Person,
- Traditional Pirates: You are a pirate in control of a little ship and have the end goal of taking over other ships and managing your crew in order to rule over the Seven Seas.  You must accumulate doubloons and crew members, may hire pirates, may conquer other ships, but also have to defend your fleet
- Tasks: Repairman, Cook, Navigator, Cannon People
- Be able to shift perspectives between ships, will tell you when ships are under attack and you will be able to appoint people as captain in your stead (Shifting perspective via firing yourself from a cannon onto another ship)
- Limbs can be cut off, right hand default but switches to left when right is cut off, can repair limbs by either herb guy giving magic potion or magic ritual by wizards
- Upgrade system: herb guy gives strength or resilience upgrades
- Stop by islands and get wood to get ship repair material
